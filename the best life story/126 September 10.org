* 126 September 10
#+begin_verse
I met him that morning in Central Park.
He had been away at school for what had seemed like an eternity.
There was no agenda.
We picnicked.
We talked about the future.
We played a game in which we kissed while sitting on every bench that we encountered.
He held my hand and said my face was more radiant than the sunshine upon us
  and my lips were softer than the cool afternoon breeze.
He would start his new job in Lower Manhattan the following day, but he set that day aside for me.
I will always remember that beautiful day, September 10, 2001.
#+end_verse
