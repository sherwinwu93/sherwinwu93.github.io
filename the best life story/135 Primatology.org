* 135 Primatology
#+begin_verse
"No.
Not ape.
That's monkey."
She's two-and-a-half, and the one thing she knows for sure is
  that the rhesus monkey at the zoo is an ape.
Maybe she'd get away misidentifying primates if she had a different mom.
I whisper, "I have a degree in physical anthropology and I'm telling you that's a monkey.
He has a tail.
Apes don't have tails."
I look around, relieved that no one heard me debating with a toddler.
She pats me on the shoulder and condescends beyond her years.
"No, Mom.
That's a monkey."
I've met my match, or rather I made her.
I'm on the other side of myself now.
I spent the first thirty years of my life correcting people,
  and now I'll spend the next thirty being corrected.
I deserve it, but my poor husband.
He didn't ask for two of me.
#+end_verse
