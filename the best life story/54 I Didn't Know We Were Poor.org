* 54 I Didn't Know We Were Poor
#+begin_verse
I was born in the hills of Tennessee.
There were five of us in our family.
We had no indoor plumbing or furnaces.
We got our water from the well and heated it on a coal-burning stove in the kitchen.
Mom used that same stove to cook on.
Our home had two fireplaces.
At night Mom would heat our beds with a bed warmer.
We took our baths in the kitchen in a tin barrel.
Mom made all our candy and cakes from scratch.
Our food was grown in our garden or my dad killed it hunting.
Mom made our clothes, each stitch with love.
Our home was always warm, we were never dirty, and our bellies were always full.
No one ever went hungry.
We always had enough no matter how many sat down at our table.
Funny, I never knew we were poor.
#+end_verse
