* 114 Out of the Blue
#+begin_verse
As a child, I cherished a close personal relationship with God.
Life went on and, as it often does,
  led me down a series of twisted and winding roads
    that ultimately led me farther away from that beloved bond.
I became a mother at the tender age of nineteen,
  and it never occurred to me to introduce my son, AJ, to someone who had meant so much to me.
One day, when AJ was four years old, he climbed into my lap as he often did.
He wrapped his tiny arms around my neck and said to me out of the blue,
  "When I was in Heaven, before I was born,
    I saw you and told God I want you to be my mommy
      'cause you were the beautifulest lady on Earth."
Obviously, they had already met.
#+end_verse
