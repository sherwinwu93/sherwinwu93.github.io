* 149 The Man with Cancer
#+begin_verse
I overheard a conversation once: 'You ever sell that condo?
You know, the one that belonged to the man with cancer?'
I wondered, 'What is the man with cancer's name?
What story does he have?'
I wondered, 'When people talk about my dad, do they call him 'the man with cancer?''
He has a name.
He has a story.
We are all people with names and stories.
We say we know this, yet somehow we are still labeled.
Left me to wonder, 'What do people see when they look at me?'
#+end_verse
