* 68 A Daredevil Nun
#+begin_verse
One day while performing the weekly chore of mowing my two acres of grass on my riding lawnmower,
  two nuns from a nearby monastery walked by and stopped to chat.
One of them said, 'I've always wanted to drive a tractor.'
To make her wish come true, I offered to let her drive mine.
I set it at the lowest speed, and she drove off.
I remarked to the other nun, 'It's like driving a car.'
To which she replied, 'She's never driven a car.'
Looking up, I saw she was headed straight for the fire hydrant.
I ran after her yelling, 'Turn it!
Turn it!'
I heard the other nun say, 'She can't hear, either.'
#+end_verse
