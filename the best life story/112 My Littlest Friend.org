* 112 My Littlest Friend
#+begin_verse
My littlest friend had long silver whiskers, black shoe button eyes, and an always-wagging tail.
He was a Kleenex stealer, a carpet wetter, and the sunshine on a rainy day.
He was never mad and always ready to go for a walk.
He was intrigued with everything, and his eyes always asked the same question,
  'Did you ever see THAT before?'
It made no difference to him whether we were pretty or ugly, rich or poor;
  he loved us unconditionally.
This is God's love in action.
He put man's best friend on Earth to give us a loving companion
  and one to look at us through eyes not blinded by worldly distractions.
Part of my heart will always miss my littlest friend, but more love is there because of him.
Sad though I am, I wouldn't have missed knowing him for the world!
#+end_verse
