* 43 The Death Race
#+begin_verse
Running alone in the Canadian Rockies and about halfway through an eighty-mile ultramarathon,
  I realized I was in over my head.
I figured nothing could be worse than taking another step, so I stopped.
At that moment I knew I had a very simple decision to make:
  I could keep my feet moving, or I could give up.
I kept moving.
Miles and mountain ranges passed beneath my feet,
  and twenty-two hours after I began, I finished the Canadian Death Race.
A few months later, we celebrated my grandpa's 100th birthday.
Hoping he might reveal some well-kept secret to longevity, I asked him,
  'Grandpa, how did you do it?'
'What, this?' he replied, deftly spinning my aunt in time with the music.
'It's simple; you just have to keep your feet moving!'
#+end_verse
