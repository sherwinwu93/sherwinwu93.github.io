* 97 Worst Enemy
#+begin_verse
Looking across the crowd at my fortieth high-school reunion,
  I spotted walking my way one of the persons I really had not looked forward to seeing.
He had been a tormenter, a teaser, a prankster who had made me feel miserable.
He grinned, called out my name, and started talking about himself
  and asking questions of me as warmly as the exchanges with long-lost best friends.
That's when I realized he really hadn't been among my worst enemies,
  for the teasing and taunting had prepared me for the rough patches in life that always arise.
Without him, I would not have been as prepared for adversity.
He was really among my best friends.
I can't wait to see him again.
#+end_verse
