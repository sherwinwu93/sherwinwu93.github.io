* 27 An Even Swap
#+begin_verse
When I got engaged in my early twenties,
  my wife-to-be was a soft-spoken young lady
    who rarely raised her voice or spouted off a retort,
    regardless of the often-snarky remarks I made about one thing or another.
One day, I asked her why WE got engaged but SHE got a diamond ring.
She said, "Well, you're getting me."
I said, "But you're getting me!"
"Right," she replied, just as calm as you please.
"And you had to put up a diamond ring to make it an even swap."
#+end_verse
