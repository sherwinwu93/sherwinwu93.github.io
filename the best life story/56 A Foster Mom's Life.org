* 56 A Foster Mom's Life
#+begin_verse
I remember the smell of her hair when she snuggled close,
  trying to crawl back into the womb from which she did not come.
I can feel her fragile arms wrapped around mine,
  her soft cheek on my chest as she held on for life.
I mean I can feel her.
I hear her beautiful broken voice soar with glee
  because she was simple and the most basic things brought her such peace.
I feel her sweet breath against my face when she whispered in my ear that she loved me.
Her desperate hands clutching my shoulders like it was a pivotal moment in time
  that we both needed to remember.
And I do.
I remember every moment of the two years she was with us.
I remember the way my soul felt the day I had to give her back.
And how it aches still.
#+end_verse
