* 44 Meeting My Father
#+begin_verse
My father passed away when I was a baby.
I never had a chance to meet him or hear his words...until thirty-four years later,
  when my mom passed away.
While going through her apartment
    and deciding what to keep, what to give away or discard,
  I would make an amazing find!
I came across a stack of about eighty letters that my father had written to her during World War II,
  when he was stationed in Europe.
I had never come across anything written by my father, so this was a precious gift.
For the first time in my life, I had a sense of my father...who he was...what his thoughts were.
Finally, I had a feeling of completion.
His letters hold a special place in my heart
  and are among my most valuable possessions for they gave me my father.
#+end_verse
