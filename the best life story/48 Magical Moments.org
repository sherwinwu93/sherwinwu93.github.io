* 48 Magical Moments
#+begin_verse
Divorced with two small children, I somehow wound up in Las Vegas in the early seventies.
It hadn't been my particular life's dream
  to wind up working in a casino or later as one of the town's first female craps dealers.
But what a spot to meet so many different people from so many walks of life.
I learned to treat normal people as celebrities and celebrities as normal people.
It worked.
My oddest moment was looking up one day
  to catch Stevie Wonder playing at a slot machine with the help of an aide
    while Ray Charles passed behind him with the help of his aide,
  and neither one of the musical giants had any idea the other was there.
There were a lot of magical moments in Vegas.
#+end_verse
