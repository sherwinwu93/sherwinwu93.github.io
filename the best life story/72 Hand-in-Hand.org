* 72 Hand-in-Hand
#+begin_verse
Many significant moments in my life have been marked by the holding of hands.
My mother held my hand as she walked me into school on my first day of kindergarten.
My soon-to-be husband held my hand as we walked across the Brooklyn Bridge.
The tiny hand of each of my children curled around my index finger right after they were born.
There was no moment more memorable, though, than the time I held the hand of my daughter
  and she couldn't return my caress.
As she lay in the hospital bed, I held her hand all night, anxious for a response.
Now, her little hand can hold mine again.
We are determined to go hand-in-hand into the future, grateful for every moment.
#+end_verse
