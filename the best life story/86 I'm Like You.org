* 86 I'm Like You
#+begin_verse
I'm like you.
I wake up each day and find the courage to live.
I love to laugh, love, dance, and sing.
I strive to do and be my best, to help others, to be healthy, to be alive.
I have goals.
Some I've accomplished and others, well, I'm trying.
Sometimes I fail, but I keep trying because I know it's worth it.
I'm worth it.
My daughter is worth it.
My life is worth living.
I want to be happy.
I want you to be happy.
Like you, I have days when I need to push through the pain, pull myself up, face my fears,
  and forgive myself for not trying hard enough yesterday.
I promise to try harder today.
Like you, I dream big dreams and I hope, wish, and pray that those dreams come true.
I believe anything is possible.
I really do.
And I'm like you.
#+end_verse
