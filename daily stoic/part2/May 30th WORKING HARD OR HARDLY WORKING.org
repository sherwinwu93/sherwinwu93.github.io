  43 hat are the chances that the busiest person you know is actually the most productive?
We tend to associate busyness with goodness and believe that spending many hours at work should be rewarded.
Instead,
  evaluate what you are doing,
  why you are doing it,
  and where accomplishing it will take you.
If you don't have a good answer,
  then stop.
